About DUDU FOOD

GOOD FOOD. GREAT MOOD.

DUDU FOOD is a local fast-food destination in Ramnagar Colony, Chittoor. We serve popular Indian and Indo-Chinese street-style favourites, from fried rice to Gobi and Hakka Manchurian.

Come in for a quick bite or send your order ahead on WhatsApp. Either way, we want every visit to feel easy and friendly.
